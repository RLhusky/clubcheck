ClubCheck

Club attendance that takes itself. Walk into a meeting and your phone checks you in.

  • Automatic check-in. Members are checked in over Bluetooth when they walk in, or by scanning a QR code.
  • Find clubs. Browse UChicago clubs, join in one tap, and get a reminder before each meeting.
  • For officers. Start or schedule meetings, see who came, track attendance, and share the list.

Sign in with Apple or Google. Free for students and clubs.
