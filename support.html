I wasn’t checked in automatically

Make sure Bluetooth is on and auto check-in is on in the You tab. If it still doesn’t work, ask an officer for the Join QR code and scan it from Home.

It says I’m checking in on another phone

Only one phone can check you in. Open ClubCheck on the phone you want to use and tap “Use this phone”.

How do I register my club?

Open My clubs and tap “Register a club”. Once it’s approved, you can schedule meetings and add officers.

How do I delete my account?

Go to You → Delete account. See the privacy policy for what’s deleted.
