Privacy Policy

ClubCheck · Last updated October 13, 2026

ClubCheck takes attendance at student club meetings. This page explains what it collects, why, and how to delete it.

What we collect

  • Your account: the name and email you enter, and an account ID from Sign in with Apple or Google. We never see your Apple or Google password.
  • Your clubs: clubs you join, register, or help run, and meetings officers schedule.
  • Check-ins: which meetings you attended, when, and how you were checked in (automatically, by QR code, or by an officer).
  • Your phone: a random ID ClubCheck creates for your phone, so only one phone can check you in.

Sign in with Google

If you sign in with Google, ClubCheck receives only your name, email address, and Google account ID. We use them only to create your account and sign you in. We don’t access your Gmail, contacts, calendar, or any other Google data, and we don’t share Google user data with anyone.

Bluetooth and location

With auto check-in on, your phone listens over Bluetooth for a nearby officer’s phone running a meeting. It only sends anything when it hears one, and then it sends only the meeting’s code. ClubCheck does not track where you are.

When an officer taps “Start now”, their phone looks up the name of the building they’re in to label the meeting. Only that place name is saved.

Who can see your information

  • Officers of a club can see the name and email of people who check in to that club’s meetings, and can share the attendance list.
  • Everyone can see club names, meeting times and places, and how many people are at a meeting. They can’t see who.
  • The ClubCheck admin can see totals and approves new clubs.

We don’t sell your information, show ads, or track you across other apps. Data is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase.

Notifications

Meeting reminders are scheduled on your phone. You can turn them off for any club, or in your phone’s Settings.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to You → Delete account. Your profile, check-in history, and phone ID are deleted right away. Clubs you own pass to another officer.
